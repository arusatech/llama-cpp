    ↓ (LlmProvider interface)

    ↓ (postMessage)

    ↓ (wasm-bindgen exports)

    ↓ (extern "C")

    ↓ (C function calls)

NATIVE STACK:
  App Code (TypeScript)
    ↓ (LlmProvider interface)
  NativeProvider
    ↓ (Capacitor bridge)
  iOS/Android Plugin
    ↓ (native bindings)
  Real llama.cpp (C/C++)
